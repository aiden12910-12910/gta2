GTA2 in your browser

Choose your installed GTA2 folder (the one containing data). Your game files are read locally and never leave this computer.

How to play

  1. Choose how to provide your GTA2 files below.
  2. Wait for the files to load and pass validation, then the game will start.
  3. Use WASD or the arrow keys to move; click the game view to enable mouse control.
